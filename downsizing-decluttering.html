Before you start, it helps to have somewhere nearby for:

  • Keep
  • Give / Sell / Donate
  • Dispose / Recycle
  • Decide Later

"Decide Later" simply means moving an item out of the area you're sorting into a separate place for later decisions, rather than putting it back where it came from.

Start with one small section. Deal with the easy decisions first and leave anything difficult for later.

Put on some music, a podcast or whatever makes the job more pleasant. Grab a drink if you want one.
